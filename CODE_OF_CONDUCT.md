# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in the AEraLogIn project should be respectful, constructive and focused on the technical work.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, contact the project maintainer privately through the repository's available contact mechanisms.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions or restrict participation when behavior violates this code of conduct.
